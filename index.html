What question are you trying to answer?

Choose by inferential question. Observed-data tools evaluate candidate effects against a reported estimate and confidence interval; design tools evaluate repeated-study behavior under assumed true effects.

All calculations happen in the linked client-side apps. This catalog performs no statistical calculation and sends no user values anywhere.

“Software validated” means numerical and release conformance to the declared formulas and tests. It does not mean clinical validation or suitability for clinical decision support. See the portfolio validation report.
